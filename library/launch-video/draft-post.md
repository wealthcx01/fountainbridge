# Draft post — ARCA launch

**Nothing here can send this.** It is a file in a repository. Posting is an external action and waits
on a recorded human approval (non-negotiable 4); the ratified GTM research goes further and makes
LinkedIn agent-drafted and human-sent. There is no connector, token or scheduler anywhere in the
piece that made the video, and adding one would put a credential next to a rendering pipeline for no
reason.

So this is a draft for a person to read, change, and post themselves.

---

## For LinkedIn

> Most "premium" in software is paint.
>
> We built ARCA the other way round. The gold in our interface appears in exactly three places, and
> every one of them means the same thing: we have proven something about that row of data. A score
> above 80. A graded holding. A price we can actually stand behind.
>
> Everywhere else — every number, every row, every chart — is restrained on purpose.
>
> It is a small rule and it decides a lot. If the accent colour has to be earned, you cannot fake
> depth with styling. You have to have the data.
>
> ARCA is a graded-card terminal: live auction data, price history, and the decks collectors build
> from them.

## Notes for whoever posts it

- The video is 13.5 seconds and silent. Both platforms autoplay muted, so it reads without sound by
  design — there is no score and nothing to turn up.
- The still at `arca-launch-still.png` is the frame the film is about. If only one image can be used,
  use that one.
- **Nothing in this draft claims a number ARCA has not published.** The figures in the film are
  illustrative of shape, not quoted as market data, and the post does not repeat them. Keep it that
  way: a launch post is the worst place to invent a statistic.
