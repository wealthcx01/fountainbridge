# ARCA-6 — Price history on the card page

**Status:** In progress · **Phase:** 1 · **Branch:** `arca-6-price-history`

## Why this matters

A collector deciding whether to sell wants to see what a card has been worth, not only what it is
worth today.

## Scope

- A twelve-month price line on the card page.
- Nothing about alerts — that is a separate ask.

## Done looks like

- [ ] The card page shows a price line for the last twelve months.
- [ ] A card with no history says so rather than drawing an empty chart.
